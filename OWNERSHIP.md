Date: 5 October 2026

aur isse jude saare rights Arun Agarhari ke hain.**

- Poora source code unko diya ja chuka hai — woh ise dekh sakte hain, badal sakte hain,

  ya isme se koi bhi hissa apne doosre project me use kar sakte hain.
- Is par koi trial, subscription, payment ya account system nahi hai — woh jaisa chahein waisa rakhein.
